Security policy for this site

This is a personal portfolio, but it is a security portfolio, so it is held to the standard it describes. If you find a weakness in it, I want to hear about it.

Scope

  • Everything served from this origin: the HTML, CSS, JavaScript, headers and the files under /.well-known/.
  • The contact form, including attempts to bypass the honeypot or inject content into submissions.

Out of scope

  • Netlify, the hosting platform, and its own infrastructure. Report platform issues to Netlify directly.
  • Denial of service, volumetric testing and automated scanning that degrades the site for others.
  • Social engineering, phishing or physical attacks against the subject or anyone connected to him.
  • Reports that a header is "missing" on a local or third-party mirror; see the verification panel for what production sends.

How to report

  1. Email the address listed in security.txt with a subject starting with [security].
  2. Include the URL, the steps to reproduce, and what you believe the impact is. A proof of concept is welcome; exfiltrating real data is not needed.
  3. If the report is sensitive, encrypt it to the PGP key referenced from security.txt once it is published.

What to expect

  • Acknowledgement within 7 days.
  • A fix or a written explanation within 30 days for anything confirmed.
  • Credit on this page, if you want it, once the issue is closed.

Safe harbour

Research carried out in good faith, within the scope above, that avoids privacy violations, data destruction and service disruption, will not lead to legal action from the subject. If in doubt, ask first.
